Publication Agency Fee

A simple per-assignment fee. No monthly subscription.

£5 per completed article assignment

How it works

  • The publication pays the freelancer's agreed assignment fee separately.
  • The publication also pays Linebreak Media a £5 agency fee for each completed article assignment.
  • The freelancer receives 100% of their agreed assignment fee.
  • The £5 agency fee is paid separately to Linebreak Media.
  • There is no monthly subscription.
  • The freelancer's assignment fee is agreed separately according to the assignment.
50% OFF YOUR FIRST FIVE ASSIGNMENTS Founding publications receive a 50% reduction on the Linebreak agency fee for their first five completed article assignments.

Founding publication rates

  • Standard agency fee: £5 per completed article assignment.
  • Founding agency fee: £2.50 per completed article assignment.
  • After the first five completed assignments, the standard £5 fee applies.
