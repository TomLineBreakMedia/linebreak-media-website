BECOME A FOUNDING CONTRIBUTOR

As BREAKMATCH Sports Media enters its launch phase, we're inviting prospective contributors to express their interest in becoming part of our founding network.

WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • Specialist knowledge of one or more sports.
  • Experience covering local, regional, national or international competitions.

  • An interest in developing their portfolio and taking on freelance opportunities.
