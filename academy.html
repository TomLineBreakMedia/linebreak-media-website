WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

TEN FOUNDATIONS. PRACTICAL SKILLS. REAL-WORLD APPLICATION.

The Linebreak Journalism Training Programme covers the essential skills needed to develop as a modern sports journalist and digital content creator. From writing and reporting to pitching, interviewing and building your own brand, each session is designed to give you practical knowledge you can put into action.

01

FINDING THE STORY: NEWS, RESEARCH AND STORYTELLING

Learn how to identify interesting stories, recognise newsworthy angles, research effectively and find opportunities beyond the scoreline. Explore news values, human-interest stories, sporting milestones and how to turn an idea into a compelling story.

02

WRITING FOR DIGITAL: ARTICLES, HEADLINES AND SEO

Develop the skills to write engaging online news articles, match reports and sports content. Learn how to structure articles, write effective introductions and headlines, use keywords, understand search intent and make content accessible to online audiences.

03

MATCH REPORTING AND LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE

Learn how to prepare for sporting events, take effective notes, identify key moments and produce accurate match reports under pressure. Cover live reporting, player ratings, post-match reaction, press-box preparation and working to tight deadlines.

04

INTERVIEWING AND PRESENTING TO CAMERA

Develop confidence when interviewing athletes, coaches and other sporting figures, both in person and on camera. Learn how to prepare questions, present confidently, deliver pieces to camera and develop skills in sports presenting, vlogging and influencer-style content.

05

FEATURES, LONG-FORM WRITING AND DIGITAL STORYTELLING

Explore how to move beyond match reports and create engaging features, profiles and longer-form sports stories. Learn about narrative structure, scene-setting, interviewing, storytelling techniques and building a compelling article from an idea.

06

EDITING, ACCURACY AND EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Understand how to edit your own work and produce professional, reliable content. Cover grammar, structure, fact-checking, research, identifying errors, avoiding repetition, editorial judgement and maintaining accuracy across written and digital content.

07

CREATING SPORTS GRAPHICS WITH CANVA

Learn how to use Canva to produce professional-looking sports graphics and visual content. Create matchday announcements, team selections, scorecards, player profiles, statistics graphics and promotional posts. Explore templates, typography, colour, branding and visual consistency.

08

SHORT-FORM VIDEO: FILMING AND EDITING FOR TIKTOK AND REELS

Learn how to plan, film and edit short-form sports content for platforms such as TikTok and Instagram Reels. Cover vertical video, hooks, sequencing, trimming clips, transitions, captions, subtitles, audio and accessible editing tools.

09

SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY AND AUDIENCE ENGAGEMENT

Understand how to build an effective social media presence around a sport, club, publication or personal brand. Explore platform differences, content calendars, scheduling, captions, audience engagement, growing an online following and adapting content for different platforms.

10

BUILDING A JOURNALISM AND CONTENT CREATION CAREER

Learn how to turn your skills into opportunities. Cover building a portfolio, pitching publications, approaching clubs and organisations, networking, developing a personal brand, finding freelance work and understanding the practical responsibilities of working independently.
